# Nexa — One-Pager

**Nexa is the lead operating system for teams that hate lead chaos.**

Capture forms and referral links → automatic scoring → priority queue → short daily action list → HubSpot sync.

No solar branding. No personal brand. Any agency, any vertical.

---

## The problem it solves

Leads come in from forms, partners, and content.  
They sit in a spreadsheet or a CRM that nobody opens.  
High-intent people go cold. Reps waste time on low-score noise.

Nexa turns that into a **Today** list of 3–5 things that actually matter.

---

## Core loop

1. Lead lands (public form or referral code)
2. Score is calculated (explainable points)
3. Lead appears in inbox + pipeline
4. If score ≥ 40 (or newest unworked), it lands on **Today**
5. Rep works it → logs action → status updates → optional sequence enrollment
6. HubSpot contact is created or updated
